-

The first massive clash occurred over the Keepstar's armor timer on December 30th and 31st YC122 with a combined 5,158 pilots fighting in the system at its peak. Supercapital fleets from both sides traded blows relatively evenly as ships exploded all around the Citadel. When the dust settled from this first battle, 3,404 capsuleer ships including 257 titans and a combined value of over 29 trillion ISK had been destroyed.
+ YC122年，通称“The Imperium”和“PAPI”的克隆飞行员联盟群之间爆发了大规模冲突，震撼了新伊甸南部和西部的0.0地区。到YC122年末，战争的主要前线是绝地之域，PAPI联盟群的军队入侵了Imperium的首都地区。同年10月，PAPI在FWST-8企图建立滩头阵地，双方的冲突打破了新伊甸历史上最大规模战斗的记录，不到三个月后，这一记录注定会再次被打破。
  
  
- 
- The second battle of M2-XFE a few days later saw even more capsuleers attempt to join the fray, leading to an infamous result. On January 3rd YC123 a record 6,739 pilots entered the system, and several thousand more attempted to join the fighting. This unprecedented number of warp-capable starships in close proximity led to unpredictable spacetime anomalies as the universe itself seemed to strain under the stress of the battle. The anomalies played havoc on all ship systems within M2, but the defending forces of the Imperium coalition were in a better position to weather the storm as they had arrived on location earlier then the PAPI fleets and therefore they did not need to make use of jump drives and stargates during the period of greatest disruption. The result of this second battle was a significant victory for the defending forces, as the attackers faced significant disruption to the operation of their jump drives into the crowded system. Many attacking ships were destroyed as they entered the system, and many more were trapped in the system by the forces of the Imperium. The Keepstar had been saved for a time.
+ 到12月底，绝地之域的战斗集中在M2-XFE星系，在那里Imperium的一个星城遭到了PAPI部队的持续攻击。首次大规模交战发生在YC122年12月30日至31日，起因是这座星城的装甲增强模式计时器，峰值共有5,158名飞行员在该星系内参战。双方的超级旗舰舰队在堡垒周围激烈交火，飞船此起彼伏地爆炸，双方势均力敌。当第一场战斗尘埃落定时，包括257艘泰坦在内的3,404艘克隆飞行员舰船被摧毁，总价值超过29万亿ISK。
  
  
+ 几天后，在M2-XFE的第二次战斗中，更多的克隆飞行员试图加入战斗，导致了一个臭名昭著的结果。YC123年1月3日，创纪录的6,739名飞行员进入该星系，另有数千人试图加入战斗。数量空前具备跃迁能力的星舰聚集在如此近的距离内，导致了难以预料的时间空间异常现象，宇宙本身似乎也在这场战斗的压力下变得紧张起来。这种异常现象对M2范围内的所有飞船系统都造成了严重破坏，但因为Imperium联盟群的防御部队比PAPI舰队更早到达战场，因此能够更好地抵御这场风暴——在干扰最剧烈的时期，他们无需使用跳跃引擎和星门。第二次战斗的结果是防御方取得了重大胜利，因为进攻方进入拥挤星系时，跳跃引擎运行受到了严重干扰。许多进攻舰队在进入星系时便被摧毁，还有更多的被Imperium部队困在星系中。星城暂时得以保全。
  
- In the weeks following the second battle of M2-XFE, Imperium fleets anchored mobile warp disruptors and patrolled relentlessly to keep as many PAPI supercapitals as possible trapped in the system as fighting raged on across the Delve region, taking advantage of the strategic advantage provided by the trapping of so many hostile vessels. Several smaller clashes occurred over operations to rescue some of the trapped supercapital vessels, and the coalitions also continued to fight over other structures in the system. On January 27th YC123, PAPI were able to free a portion of their trapped pilots after a battle that saw nearly 450 Capitals and 6 Titans-class hulls destroyed.  On February 1st YC123, PAPI forces were victorious in a battle that stretched across the NJU-QV constellation and involved thousands of capsuleers, giving them control of M2-XFE's Infrastructure Hub. PAPI then began to slowly lay the groundwork to activate their own Tenebrex Cyno Jammer structure which would give them the ability to disrupt Imperium reinforcements to the system. On March 7th YC123 the PAPI Cyno Jammer structure came online and the Imperium retreated the bulk of their forces from the system, freeing the remaining trapped PAPI capital ships. Finally on March 12th YC123 the M2-XFE Keepstar that had been the original focal point of all this bloodshed was destroyed by PAPI forces.
  
-  
+ 在M2-XFE第二次战役之后的几周里，Imperium舰队利用困住如此多敌对舰船所带来的战略优势，在绝地之域各地战火纷飞之时，锚定了机动跃迁扰断器并进行了不懈的巡逻，以尽可能多地将PAPI的超级旗舰困在该星系中。在营救一些被困的超级旗舰的过程中发生了几次小规模冲突，两个联盟群还在继续争夺星系中的其他建筑。YC123年1月27日，PAPI在经历了一场摧毁了近450艘旗舰和6艘泰坦的战斗后，成功解救了部分被困飞行员。YC123年2月1日，PAPI部队在一场横跨NJU-QV星座、数千名克隆飞行员参与的战斗中取得胜利，控制了M2-XFE的基础设施中心。随后，PAPI开始慢慢为启动他们自己的暗影诱导干扰塔建筑打下基础，这将使他们有能力干扰Imperium对该星系的增援。YC123年3月7日，PAPI的诱导干扰塔启动，Imperium随即从该星系撤出了大部分部队，释放了剩余被困的PAPI旗舰。最后，在YC123年3月12日，这起血腥冲突最初的焦点——M2-XFE星城被PAPI的部队摧毁。
  
- All in all, the fighting within M2-XFE in late YC122 and early YC123 led to the destruction of more 20,000 capsuleer vessels including over 450 titan-class starships. The first battle of M2-XFE broke the New Eden record for the most value destroyed in a single battle, and the second battle broke FWST-8's record for the battle with the most capsuleer ships within a system at one time. The Keepstar was eventually destroyed after over two months of brutal fighting, and the forces of the Imperium regrouped at their nearby capital system of 1DQ1-A. At the time that this monument was erected, the greater war between PAPI and the Imperium was raging on and only time would tell how the conflict would eventually be resolved.
+ 
+ 总而言之，在YC122年末和YC123年初的M2-XFE星系中的战斗中，有超过20,000艘克隆飞行员舰船被摧毁，其中包括450多艘泰坦。M2-XFE的第一场战斗打破了新伊甸单场战斗摧毁价值最高的记录，而第二场战斗则打破了FWST-8星系内同时出现最多克隆飞行员舰船的记录。经过两个多月的残酷战斗，星城最终被摧毁，Imperium部队在其附近的首都星系1DQ1-A重新集结。在这座纪念碑竖立之时，PAPI和Imperium之间的全面大战仍在激烈地进行，这场冲突最终将如何解决，唯有时间能给出答案。
